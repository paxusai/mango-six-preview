About Mango Six

A Korean dessert café, under warm track lights.

MangoSix is a Korean café brand built as a “dessert department store”: coffee, mango juices, bingsu, smoothies, bubble tea, pretzels and pancakes under one roof.

The Saipan café on Route 31 in Dandan is one of the largest and most spacious on the island — reviewers call it the “largest coffee shop on island” (Yelp). Charcoal walls, warm wood and olive leather make it a calm room for studying, meeting friends or “a last stop on the way to the airport” (Google).
